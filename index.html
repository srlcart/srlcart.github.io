FAQ

What is the delivery time?
Original paper cut artworks usually take 7–14 working days to finish. Prints will be shipped within 3–5 working days after payment is confirmed. Custom commissions will have a separate timeline agreed before starting.
How much is shipping?
Local Hong Kong shipping: HK$30. International shipping will be calculated based on destination and package weight, and quoted after the order is placed.
Can I request a custom paper cut commission?
Yes. Please describe your idea, size, and preferred elements in the order note. Our student artists will reply with details, a quotation, and a timeline.
Return & Refund Policy
Original handmade paper cuts are non-refundable unless damaged during delivery. Please provide photo proof within 3 days after receiving the parcel.
Payment methods
We accept PayMe and FPS (Hong Kong). Payment details will be shown after you submit your order.
